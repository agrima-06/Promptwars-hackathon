## 1. Phase I: Houselisting & Housing Amenities (31 Parameters)

### Q1: What are the 31 questions asked in Phase I of Census 2027?
**Answer:** Phase I captures 31 vital parameters across 5 socio-economic categories:
1. **Building & Structure Identification**: Building Number, Census House Number, Predominant material of Floor, Wall, and Roof (Pucca, Semi-Pucca, Kutcha).
2. **House Use & Ownership**: Predominant use of census house (Residential, Residence-cum-work, Commercial, Institutional), Condition of census house (Good, Livable, Dilapidated), Household Number, Ownership status (Owned, Rented, Any other).
3. **Living Accommodation**: Number of dwelling rooms exclusively in possession of the household, Number of married couples residing.
4. **Drinking Water & Sanitation (Jal Jeevan & Swachh Bharat)**: Main source of drinking water (Treated tap water, Untreated tap, Covered well, Handpump, Spring/River), Location of water source (Within premises, Near premises, Away), Source of lighting (Grid electricity, Grid-tied Rooftop Solar - PM Surya Ghar, Kerosene), Latrine access and type (Flush connected to piped sewer, Flush connected to septic tank, Twin pit latrine, Community/Public latrine), Waste water outlet connectivity (Closed drainage, Open drainage, No drainage).
5. **Kitchen & Clean Cooking Energy (PM Ujjwala)**: Cooking facility (Separate kitchen inside, Open cooking), Main cooking fuel (LPG/PNG piped, Electricity/Induction, Bio-gas, Firewood/Cowdung, Kerosene).
6. **Household Assets & Digital Connectivity**: Availability of Radio/Transistor, Television (Smart TV/DTH), Internet Connection (Broadband/Wi-Fi/Fiber), Laptop/Computer/Tablet, 4G/5G Smartphone, Telephone/Mobile, Two-Wheeler (Scooter/Motorcycle/EV 2W), Four-Wheeler (Car/Jeep/EV 4W), Primary Cereal consumed.

### Q2: How is the material of wall and roof recorded? What is a Pucca vs. Kutcha house?
**Answer:**
- **Pucca House**: Dwellings whose walls and roofs are constructed with permanent, durable materials. Walls: Burnt bricks, stone blocks, reinforced concrete, or galvanized iron. Roof: Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC), tiles, timber with concrete, or machine-made tiles.
- **Semi-Pucca House**: Houses with fixed walls of brick/stone but roofs made of temporary materials (thatch/asbestos/tin), or vice versa.
- **Kutcha House**: Structures made with traditional non-durable materials (mud, unburnt bricks, thatch, bamboo, reed, leaves, or plastic sheets).

### Q3: How does Census 2027 track Rooftop Solar (PM Surya Ghar) and Clean Energy?
**Answer:** Under Question 14 of Phase I, for the first time in Indian Census history, enumerators record whether the household utilizes **Grid-Connected Rooftop Solar Energy** under the *PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana*, standalone off-grid solar kits, or standard grid electricity.

## 2. Phase II: Population Demographics & Caste Enumeration

### Q4: When is Phase II conducted, and what demographic details are collected?
**Answer:** Phase II (Population Enumeration) will be conducted across the nation from **February 9 to February 28, 2027** (with a revision round from March 1 to 5, 2027). In snowbound non-synchronous areas (Ladakh, higher reaches of Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand, and parts of J&K), enumeration is conducted in September 2026.
Parameters collected include:
- Name of normal resident, Relationship to Head, Sex, Date of birth and age, Marital status, Age at marriage.
- Religion, Mother Tongue, Subsidiary languages known.
- Literacy status, Highest educational attainment, School/College attendance.
- Economic activity: Main worker (worked $\ge 6$ months), Marginal worker, Non-worker; Occupation; Industry sector; Mode of commute to work.
- Migration details: Place of birth, Place of last residence, Reason for migration, Duration of stay.

### Q5: How is Caste and Tribe enumerated in Census 2027?
**Answer:** Census 2027 records the social classification of each resident citizen:
1. **Scheduled Caste (SC)**: As notified under Article 341 of the Constitution of India.
2. **Scheduled Tribe (ST)**: As notified under Article 342 of the Constitution of India.
3. **Other Backward Classes (OBC / SEBC)**: Enumerated in accordance with Presidential orders and official State Gazette notifications.
4. **General & EWS (Economically Weaker Sections)**.
No caste certificate document needs to be presented; the self-declaration by the head of the household is recorded faithfully.

## 3. Self-Enumeration Portal & SE ID Generation

### Q6: How do citizens perform online Self-Enumeration?
**Answer:**
1. **Access Portal**: Visit the official Census 2027 portal (`censusindia.gov.in` / Self-Enumeration Portal) during the state's active 15-day pre-survey window.
2. **Mobile Authentication**: Log in using your mobile number and verify via OTP.
3. **Complete Phase I & II Schedules**: Fill in the 31 Housing parameters and household member demographic roster.
4. **Geospatial Geo-Tagging**: Tag your residence structure using browser GPS or the interactive digital map marker.
5. **SE ID Generation**: Click submit to generate your unique **Self-Enumeration ID (SE ID)** (e.g. `IND-2027-UP-LKO-849201`) with an encrypted QR code digital pass.

### Q7: What happens when the physical Census Enumerator visits my home?
**Answer:** When the official Census Enumerator visits your home during the fieldwork phase, simply show your **SE ID or QR Code Pass** on your smartphone. The enumerator scans your code using the official Census Android/iOS tablet app, validates the physical geo-coordinates in under 60 seconds, and issues a final digital receipt—without asking you all 31 questions again!

## 4. Privacy, Data Security & Section 15 Legal Immunities

### Q8: Is Census data shared with the Income Tax Department, GST, or Police?
**Answer:** **ABSOLUTELY NOT.** Under **Section 15 of the Census Act, 1948**, all records of the census are strictly confidential.
- Census records are **NOT open to inspection** by any tax authorities, police agencies, or municipal revenue departments.
- Census schedules are **INADMISSIBLE AS EVIDENCE** against any person in any court of law in civil or criminal proceedings.
- Reporting assets such as air conditioners, cars, or spacious rooms will **never** cause tax scrutiny or property tax hikes.

### Q9: Is Biometric data (Fingerprints / Iris / Facial recognition) collected in Census 2027?
**Answer:** **No.** Census 2027 does not collect any biometric information. It is strictly a socio-economic and demographic questionnaire. If any individual claiming to be an enumerator asks for biometric scans or banking credentials, report them immediately to the national toll-free helpline **1800-11-2027**.

### Q10: Does the Census link with bank accounts or ask for financial OTPs?
**Answer:** **Never.** The Census Department does not disburse direct cash rewards, subsidies, or ask for Bank Account Numbers, Debit Card CVVs, UPI PINs, or Financial OTPs. Any SMS or message claiming cash credits for census completion is a fraudulent phishing attempt.

## 5. State-Wise Schedules & Field Enumeration Logistics

### Q11: What is the 15-day pre-survey self-enumeration window?
**Answer:** Prior to physical enumerator visits in any district, ORGI opens a **15-day digital self-enumeration window** allowing citizens to complete questionnaires online at their convenience. Check the State Tracker module to see active and upcoming window dates for all 36 States & UTs.

### Q12: Is answering Census questions mandatory under Indian law?
**Answer:** Yes. Under **Section 8 of the Census Act, 1948**, every citizen is legally obligated to answer census questions truthfully. Simultaneously, under Section 11, census officials are bound by an oath of strict secrecy, with severe penalties for unauthorized data disclosure.
